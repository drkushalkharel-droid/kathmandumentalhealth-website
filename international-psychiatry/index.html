Online care from abroad

Urgent online psychiatric consultation, wherever you are

Information from Kathmandu Mental Health ClinicMedically reviewed by Dr. Kushal Kharel, Consultant Psychiatrist · Online therapyLast reviewed 27 September 2026About our clinical information

If you live outside Nepal and want to speak with a psychiatrist, contact Kathmandu Mental Health Clinic to ask about an online appointment. The clinician joins from Kathmandu; you join from the country where you are physically located. We confirm suitability, local limitations, clinician availability, the fee, and connection details before you pay. Nepali and English consultations are available.

Dr. Kushal Kharel

Consultant Psychiatrist · Online consultation and therapy · MD Neuropsychiatry · Nepal Medical Council registration No. 27199

Kathmandu-based Dr. Kharel offers video consultations in Nepali or English for people living abroad. You can discuss anxiety, constant worry, intrusive thoughts, sleep problems, low mood, and other mental health concerns. Read Dr. Kharel’s profile and clinical background. How to arrange a consultation

  1. Message us on WhatsApp with your country, time zone, preferred language, and a brief description of what support you need.
  2. Agree on a time and fee. We will confirm the doctor's availability and share the consultation fee and payment instructions directly.
  3. Pay by an agreed method. International bank transfer may be arranged; ask us whether USD, EUR or GBP is suitable for your transfer. Your bank or intermediary bank may charge fees.
  4. Join by video. We will confirm the video link and connection details after booking. Zoom may be arranged; Google Meet or WhatsApp video may also be available.

Who can contact us?

Adults living abroad can ask about a psychiatric assessment, follow-up, second opinion, or support with mental health concerns. You do not need to be Nepali to make an enquiry. Before confirming a clinical appointment, we need to know the patient’s current country and city: rules for remote medical care differ across jurisdictions, and a consultation may not be available or appropriate from every location.

We receive enquiries from the United States and Canada, the United Kingdom and countries across Europe, Singapore, Australia, New Zealand, the Gulf, and other parts of Asia. Our country guides for Nepalis abroad provide additional location-specific information.

Feeling anxious or overwhelmed while abroad?

You might describe it as “Malai chinta cha, overthinking huncha, naramailo lagcha, dar lagcha” — I feel worried, keep overthinking, feel low or afraid. You do not need to know the diagnosis before asking for support. You can ask for an online appointment with Dr. Kushal Kharel, Consultant Psychiatrist, in Nepali or English. Call +977 9714556408 or message the clinic on WhatsApp with your country and time zone. The clinic must confirm availability and whether a consultation can be offered from your location. This is not an emergency service; if you cannot stay safe, contact local emergency services now.

What happens in an online consultation?

After confirming that an appointment can be offered from your location, the clinic will tell you which clinician is available and how to connect. The clinician will ask about your concerns, relevant history, current treatment, and what you hope to get from the meeting. Together, you can discuss possible next steps, which may include follow-up, local care, or a written summary where appropriate.

Video appointments may use Zoom, Google Meet, or WhatsApp video; confirm the platform with us before paying. Join from a private room with a stable connection. For your privacy, avoid shared devices or public Wi-Fi where possible, and do not send detailed medical records or sensitive personal information over WhatsApp unless the clinic has explained a suitable secure way to share them.

Appointments are scheduled, not an instant on-demand video line. We will confirm the earliest time we can offer; a message does not guarantee an immediate or same-day appointment.

Safety assessment and family support

During an online consultation, Dr. Kharel can ask about safety concerns and assess mental health risks. If support from family members in Nepal could help, he can coordinate with them only with your consent. The information shared with family is limited to what you agree to share.

Payment from another country

Ask us for the current fee and available payment instructions before sending money. Depending on the arrangement available, payment can be made by bank transfer, and you may ask to pay in US dollars (USD), euros (EUR), or British pounds (GBP). The clinic will confirm the accepted currency, transfer details, exchange handling, and any fees with you directly.

For your security, use only bank details sent to you by the clinic through its verified contact channel. Include the patient's name or booking reference as instructed, and send proof of transfer so we can confirm receipt. Do not send payment to an unverified personal account. Bank and intermediary transfer charges may apply; check these with your bank.

Prescriptions and care where you live

Prescription and pharmacy rules depend on the country and medicine. A prescription from Nepal may not be accepted for local dispensing. If medication is part of your care, Dr. Kharel can coordinate with a local doctor when clinically appropriate. The local clinician assesses treatment and prescribing options under local rules; Dr. Kharel can help share a clinical summary where appropriate. Do not start, stop, or change prescribed medicine without speaking with your treating clinician.

Cross-border care: check your location first

Online access does not remove country-specific rules. For example, US federal telehealth guidance explains that licensing requirements vary by state and advises providers to verify a patient’s location before an appointment. Other countries have their own requirements. We therefore ask where you will physically be during the consultation and may need to decline or limit an appointment if we cannot provide it appropriately. Read the US HHS guidance on telehealth licensing.

WHO’s telehealth quality guidance highlights patient safety, privacy, accountability, and appropriate service pathways as considerations for provider organizations. A video visit also has limits: if an examination, urgent intervention, or local prescription is needed, we may recommend in-person care near you. Read WHO’s Telehealth quality of care tool.

If you need emergency help now

This clinic is not an emergency or crisis service, and messages may not be seen immediately. If you or someone else may be in immediate danger, call your local emergency number now, go to the nearest emergency department, or ask a trusted person nearby to stay with you. For a country-specific crisis line, use Find A Helpline. Do not wait for an online appointment or a reply from this clinic in an emergency.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get an appointment urgently?

Message us with your country, time zone, and availability and ask for the earliest slot. We will confirm whether an appointment is available. This is a scheduled consultation service and cannot guarantee immediate response or same-day availability.

Can I pay in USD, EUR or GBP?

Ask the clinic to confirm the current accepted currency and transfer instructions before sending payment. USD, EUR, and GBP may be arranged for international transfer; bank and intermediary fees may apply.

Do you use Zoom?

Zoom can be arranged when confirmed during booking. Depending on availability, Google Meet or WhatsApp video may also be used. We will send the connection details after confirming your appointment.

Can someone living outside Nepal book even if they are not Nepali?

Yes, you can contact us to ask about suitability and availability. Share your current country because rules for cross-border medical consultations and prescriptions vary by location.

Request an online appointment

Message us with your country and time zone. We will reply with availability, the fee, payment options, and video details.
