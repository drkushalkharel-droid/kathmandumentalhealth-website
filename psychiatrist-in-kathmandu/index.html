Psychiatrist in Kathmandu, Nepal: Locations, Areas We Serve and How to Book

By Kathmandu Mental Health ClinicUpdated 13 November 20263 min read

Kathmandu Mental Health Clinic is a psychiatry and counselling clinic in central Kathmandu. This page shows where to find us, which location is closest to your part of the Valley, and how video consultation works if travelling is hard.

Key takeaways

  • Two locations: Dev Plaza, New Road (central Kathmandu) and Kalanki Bhatbhateni (beside Malpot) (west Kathmandu).
  • Patients come from across the Kathmandu Valley, including Kathmandu, Lalitpur and Bhaktapur, and from other districts by video consultation.
  • Please call or message before you come to confirm the doctor and the time.

Where to find us in Kathmandu

  • Dev Plaza, New Road, Kathmandu (central Kathmandu, close to the New Road shopping area). Open in Google Maps.
  • Kalanki Bhatbhateni (beside Malpot), Kathmandu (west Kathmandu, next to Malpot, on the Kalanki side of the Ring Road).

Open every day, 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM. Call or message +977 971-4556408 on WhatsApp to confirm which location suits you and which doctor is available. Our clinic is an outpatient clinic: it is not a hospital and not an emergency service.

If travelling is hard, meet us by video

You do not need to travel through Kathmandu traffic for every visit. After a first assessment, many follow-ups can be done by video from home or work, in Nepali or English. See online psychiatrist in Nepal and the booking page.

What we treat in Kathmandu

Our psychiatrists assess and treat depression, anxiety, panic attacks, OCD, bipolar disorder, sleep problems, ADHD, addiction and memory concerns, with counselling, therapy, medicine or a combination.

From outside the Valley

People also consult us from Pokhara, Chitwan, Butwal, Biratnagar and other towns by video. Read about video consultation by province.

If it is an emergency

In Nepal call Police 100 or Ambulance 102, or go to the nearest hospital emergency department. The suicide prevention helpline is 1166. We are not an emergency service.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the psychiatrist clinic in Kathmandu?

Kathmandu Mental Health Clinic is at Dev Plaza, New Road, in central Kathmandu, and also at Kalanki Bhatbhateni (beside Malpot) in west Kathmandu.

Is there a psychiatrist near Thamel?

Thamel is about 1 to 3 km by road from our Dev Plaza, New Road location in central Kathmandu.

Can I visit from Lalitpur or Bhaktapur?

Yes. Patients come from across the Kathmandu Valley, including Lalitpur and Bhaktapur. Dev Plaza, New Road is roughly 4 to 6 km from central Lalitpur and 9 to 15 km from Bhaktapur, depending on traffic.

Do I need an appointment?

Please call or message us on +977 971-4556408 to confirm the doctor and a time before you come.

Do you see patients from outside Kathmandu?

Yes, by video consultation, from anywhere in Nepal or abroad.

Do you offer home visits?

On request. Availability, area, timing and fee are confirmed at booking. See our guide on home visit psychiatry in Kathmandu.
