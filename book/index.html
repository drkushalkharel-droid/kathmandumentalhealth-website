Frequently asked questions
Do I need a referral?
No. You can book directly. Doctors, schools and counsellors can also refer you.
How does booking work?
Choose a service and doctor, pick a date and a time window in your own time zone, add your details and send. Your request opens on WhatsApp so we can confirm the exact slot and send the video link.
Is the booking confirmed straight away?
The request reaches us instantly, but the slot is confirmed by a reply from the clinic, so there are no clashes. Replies are usually quick; if you do not hear back, call +977 9714556408.
What devices do I need?
A phone, tablet or laptop with a camera, a stable internet connection and headphones. We use Google Meet or WhatsApp video.
How much does it cost?
Consultation with brief counselling Rs 1,500; full counselling session Rs 2,000; CBT, CBT with ERP or systematic desensitisation Rs 2,500. We confirm the fee when we confirm your slot.
How do I pay from abroad?
We share the available payment options when we confirm your slot. A family member in Nepal can also pay on your behalf.
Can I see a particular doctor?
Yes. Choose a doctor in the booking steps, or select "No preference" and we will match you.
Which languages are available?
Nepali and English with all doctors. Hindi and Bangla with some, and Tamang with Dr. Sunita Lama.
Can I get medicine online?
The doctor can recommend treatment, including medicine when needed. Prescribing rules differ by country, so for patients abroad we coordinate with a local doctor where appropriate.
Is my information private?
Yes. Information is shared only with your consent, except where there is a serious risk to life. Please avoid writing sensitive details in the notes box.
Is this an emergency service?
No. If you or someone else is in danger, call Police 100, Ambulance 102 or the suicide prevention helpline 1166 in Nepal, or your local emergency number abroad.
Can a family member join?
Yes, if you wish, for part or all of the session. You can also attend alone.
