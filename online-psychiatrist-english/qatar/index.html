Who this is for in Qatar

This service is for adults who would like to talk to a psychiatrist in English and find it hard to do so where they are: expatriates, international students, people working abroad, travellers on long stays, and local residents who prefer English or face long waits, distance or cost. It is not for emergencies, and it does not replace a doctor who can examine you in person.

Finding psychiatric care in Qatar

Qatar is hot for much of the year, and many jobs run through long shifts or the hottest hours of the day, which can wear down sleep and mood. Days off can be short, so a video session that fits around a shift can be easier than travelling to a clinic.

Qatar uses one time zone all year, with no clock changes. A video call is hard in a shared room.

Whatever your situation, you can ask about local options at any time. With your agreement we can help you prepare a summary to share with a doctor in Qatar.

Time difference between Qatar and Nepal

Nepal is at UTC+5:45 all year, with no daylight saving.

If you finish a night shift in the morning, tell us. Early morning in Doha is mid-morning in Kathmandu, and we can look for a time around your rest.

The working week in Qatar is usually Sunday to Thursday, with Friday and Saturday as the weekend, but many workers have only one day off a week, so tell us which day is yours. During Ramadan, working hours and sleep change in many workplaces, and we can plan around them.

Frequently asked questions

Can I see an English-speaking psychiatrist online from Qatar?

Yes. Dr. Kushal Kharel, founder of Kathmandu Mental Health Clinic and a Consultant Psychiatrist (MD Neuropsychiatry, NMC No. 27199), provides online psychiatry video consultation in English for people in Qatar through Google Meet or WhatsApp video call, or by phone if video is not possible. Dr. Sunita Lama and Dr. Bhishma K.C. also provide video consultation.

I am not Nepali. Can I still book?

Yes. You do not need to be Nepali or have any link to Nepal. Anyone living, working, studying or travelling in Qatar who would like to talk to a psychiatrist in English can book. No referral is needed.

What if I cannot get a psychiatrist where I live in Qatar?

Waiting lists, distance, cost, or a lack of English-speaking clinicians can all make care hard to reach. Book with us and we will offer the earliest slot that suits your time zone. This is not a replacement for emergency care or for a local doctor who can examine you in person.
