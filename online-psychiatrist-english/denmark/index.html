Who this is for in Denmark

This service is for adults who would like to talk to a psychiatrist in English and find it hard to do so where they are: expatriates, international students, people working abroad, travellers on long stays, and local residents who prefer English or face long waits, distance or cost. It is not for emergencies, and it does not replace a doctor who can examine you in person.

Finding psychiatric care in Denmark

Denmark has long, dark winters with very short days in December, and this can lower mood for some people, especially in the first year. Danish is hard to learn quickly, and social life can revolve around groups that already exist, which can feel isolating.

Denmark is in the European Union and changes its clocks in spring and autumn. Danish social life often happens indoors and in small groups, and long winter evenings can feel very quiet if you are new.

A doctor can tell you whether more support is needed.

Whatever your situation, you can ask about local options at any time. With your agreement we can help you prepare a summary to share with a doctor in Denmark.

Frequently asked questions

Can I see an English-speaking psychiatrist online from Denmark?

Yes. Dr. Kushal Kharel, founder of Kathmandu Mental Health Clinic and a Consultant Psychiatrist (MD Neuropsychiatry, NMC No. 27199), provides online psychiatry video consultation in English for people in Denmark through Google Meet or WhatsApp video call, or by phone if video is not possible. Dr. Sunita Lama and Dr. Bhishma K.C. also provide video consultation.

I am not Nepali. Can I still book?

Yes. You do not need to be Nepali or have any link to Nepal. Anyone living, working, studying or travelling in Denmark who would like to talk to a psychiatrist in English can book. No referral is needed.

What if I cannot get a psychiatrist where I live in Denmark?

Waiting lists, distance, cost, or a lack of English-speaking clinicians can all make care hard to reach. Book with us and we will offer the earliest slot that suits your time zone. This is not a replacement for emergency care or for a local doctor who can examine you in person.
