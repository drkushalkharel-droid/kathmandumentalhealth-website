Choosing care

Psychiatrist Near Me in Kathmandu: Locations, Online Options and What to Expect

By Kathmandu Mental Health ClinicPublished 25 September 20263 min read

If you are searching for a psychiatrist near you in Kathmandu, you probably want three things: a qualified doctor, a place that is easy to reach, and clarity about what happens next. Here is how to find care close to you, and what to do if travelling is hard.

Key takeaways

  • Choose a Nepal Medical Council-registered psychiatrist at a location you can reach without stress.
  • Kathmandu Mental Health Clinic has two locations: People's Plaza (New Road) and Kalanki Bhatbhateni.
  • If travel is difficult, all three of our doctors consult online across Nepal and abroad.
  • Call ahead: doctors' schedules differ by location and day.

How to find a psychiatrist near you

Search on a map app for "psychiatrist" or "mental health clinic" and open the profiles. Look for the doctor's name and qualifications, opening hours, photos of the entrance, the phone number and recent reviews. Then call to confirm who is available and when. Ask for the doctor's NMC registration number, and if you are unsure whom to choose, read our guide to choosing a psychiatrist.

Where we are

People's Plaza (New Road): Dev Plaza, People's Plaza, New Road (नयाँ सडक), Kathmandu 44600. Central and easy to reach by public transport from across the valley.

Kalanki Bhatbhateni: Kalanki, Kathmandu, convenient if you live in the west of the city or travel along the Kalanki road.

Patients also travel to us from across the Kathmandu Valley, including Lalitpur, Bhaktapur and Balkot. Choose whichever location is easier for you, and see maps and hours on our home page.

Cannot travel? Consult online

Distance, work, illness or privacy can make a clinic visit hard. All three of our doctors offer online consultation and therapy by video call or phone, in Nepali or English, for patients anywhere in Nepal and for Nepalis abroad. Online care suits follow-ups and many first assessments. Severe symptoms, safety concerns or a need for a physical examination are better seen in person.

What to bring to your first visit

  • Any earlier prescriptions, reports and test results.
  • The names of medicines you take now (or the medicines themselves).
  • A few notes: what you are feeling, when it started, sleep, appetite, stress and family history.
  • A trusted family member or friend, if you want support.

What happens at the first appointment

Your first visit is a conversation, not a test. The doctor listens, asks about your health and life, explains what may be going on and agrees a plan with you: counselling, medicine, or both. Nothing is started without your understanding.

When it is urgent

A clinic is not an emergency service. If someone is in immediate danger or thinking of ending their life, call Nepal Police on 100, an ambulance on 102, the suicide prevention helpline 1166, or go to the nearest hospital emergency department.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an appointment?

Please call ahead. Doctors' schedules differ by location and day, and booking means you are seen without a long wait. You do not need a referral.

Is there a psychiatrist available on Saturday?

People's Plaza is open on Saturdays from 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM. Please call to confirm the doctor's availability before you come.

Can I see a psychiatrist online if I live far from Kathmandu?

Yes. All three of our doctors consult online, in Nepali or English, for patients across Nepal and abroad.

Talk to a psychiatrist

Call or message us. We reply in Nepali or English, and all three of our doctors also consult online.
