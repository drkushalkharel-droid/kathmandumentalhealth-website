How Much Does It Cost to See a Psychiatrist in Nepal? (2026 Guide)

By Kathmandu Mental Health ClinicMedically reviewed by Dr. Kushal Kharel, Consultant Psychiatrist, 8 November 2026Published 8 November 20263 min readHow we write our guides

Cost is one of the first things people ask before booking. This guide explains what a psychiatrist visit in Nepal usually involves, what affects the price, and what our own published fees are.

Key takeaways

  • Fees depend on the type of session, not only on the doctor. A first assessment takes longer than a short review.
  • At Kathmandu Mental Health Clinic a consultation with brief counselling is Rs 1,500, a full counselling session is Rs 2,000, and CBT or CBT with ERP is Rs 2,500 per session. We confirm the fee before you book.
  • Online consultation uses the same type of session, so you do not pay for travel or time off work.

What affects the fee

Psychiatrist fees in Nepal differ between hospitals, private clinics and individual doctors, and they change over time. The main things that move the price are the length of the session, whether it is a first assessment or a follow-up, whether therapy such as CBT is included, and the location.

  • Session type: a short review costs less than a long first assessment or a therapy session.
  • Therapy: structured therapy such as CBT is usually priced per session.
  • Medicine: the cost of medicines is separate from the consultation fee.
  • Tests: blood tests or scans, if needed, are paid separately.

Our published fees

We publish our fees so that you know before you book. At Kathmandu Mental Health Clinic:

  • Consultation with brief counselling: Rs 1,500
  • Full counselling session: Rs 2,000
  • CBT, CBT with ERP (for OCD) or systematic desensitisation: Rs 2,500 per session

We confirm the fee again when you book, because it depends on the session you need. See the booking page for the current details.

Paying from abroad

Nepalis abroad often ask how payment works. Tell us your country when you message us on WhatsApp and we will explain the payment options that are available at the time of booking. Family members in Nepal can often help with payment on your behalf. Do not send money before the fee and method have been confirmed with the clinic.

Is the cost worth it?

Untreated depression, anxiety or insomnia costs people sleep, work days and relationships. A single assessment can clarify whether you need counselling, medicine or both, and a clear plan often saves money compared with repeated short visits that do not address the cause. If cost is a concern, say so when you book; we will tell you honestly what you need and what can wait.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a psychiatrist consultation in Nepal?

It varies by clinic and by session. At Kathmandu Mental Health Clinic a consultation with brief counselling is Rs 1,500, and we confirm the fee before you book.

Is an online consultation cheaper?

The session fee is the same type of fee. You save the cost and time of travel, and you can join from home or work.

Do medicines cost extra?

Yes. Medicines are bought from a pharmacy and are not part of the consultation fee.

Can my family pay for me from Nepal?

Often yes. Tell us when you book and we will explain the options.
