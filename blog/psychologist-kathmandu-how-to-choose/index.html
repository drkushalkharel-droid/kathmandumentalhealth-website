Choosing mental health care

Psychologist in Kathmandu: How to Choose

Kathmandu Mental Health ClinicMedically reviewed by Dr. Kushal Kharel, Consultant PsychiatristUpdated 28 September 2026

If you are searching for the best psychologist in Kathmandu, focus on fit, training and the kind of support you need. No online list can identify one best psychologist for every person. These questions can help you compare options and decide where to begin.

What does a psychologist do?

Psychologists may provide psychological assessment, therapy and support with emotions, thoughts and behaviour. Their training and area of practice matter: ask whether their experience matches your concern, such as anxiety, depression, trauma, child development, learning or relationship difficulties. A “therapist” can refer to different kinds of providers, so check the specific qualifications instead of relying on a title alone.

A psychiatrist is a medical doctor who assesses mental health alongside medical factors and can discuss medication. Psychologists and psychiatrists may work together when a person needs both therapy and medical care.

What to ask a psychologist before booking

  • What is your qualification and professional experience? Ask about clinical training, supervision and the age groups or concerns they work with.
  • What happens in an assessment? A psychologist should explain what information they need, how they make recommendations, and whether they will provide a written report if relevant.
  • What therapy do you offer? Ask how sessions are structured, what goals you may agree on, and how you will review progress.
  • How do you handle privacy? Discuss notes, who may access information and exceptions when there is a serious safety concern or a legal requirement.
  • What are the fees and practical arrangements? Confirm session length, location, language, online availability and cancellation terms in advance.

When to consider a psychiatrist as well

Ask about psychiatric assessment if symptoms are intense or persistent, substantially affect work or study, involve serious sleep or appetite changes, include thoughts of self-harm, or may need medication or a medical review. This does not mean therapy is unnecessary. Many people use both therapy and psychiatric care, with a plan that reflects their needs and preferences.

For children, a child psychologist may help with assessment and therapy, while a child psychiatrist can assess medical and developmental factors and discuss medication when appropriate. Our guide explains when to see a child psychologist or child psychiatrist in Kathmandu.

Finding the right service in Kathmandu

Ask your doctor, school or a trusted health service for options, then contact the provider directly to check current availability and whether they offer the service you need. The clinic’s psychiatrists provide assessment and therapy including CBT; when specialised psychological assessment or therapy is a better fit, ask about referral options. See our guide to psychiatrists, psychologists and therapists.

If someone may harm themselves or another person, cannot stay safe, or is severely unwell, seek urgent in-person help through local emergency services or the nearest emergency department rather than waiting for a routine appointment.
