Free Mental Health Tools

Last updated 21 October 2026

Four simple tools you can use right now: calm your breathing, name a feeling, sort your worries, and track your mood. Nothing you enter leaves your device.

Calm the lake: guided breathing

Breathe in as the circle grows, out as it shrinks. Four counts in, six counts out. Two to five minutes can settle a racing heart.

Feelings wheel

Finding the right word takes the edge off a feeling. Pick the closest one, then a more precise one.

Circles of control

Worry grows on what you cannot change. Sort what is on your mind into what you can control, and what you cannot. Then put your energy into the first circle. Stays on your device.
