Nepalis in Australia: life, work and study

Australia is very large, and Nepalis live in every state, especially in Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Adelaide and Perth, and in regional towns. Many are international students or workers, and the distances between cities, and between you and your relatives, are long.

Australia has several time zones. Some states change their clocks for daylight saving and others, such as Queensland and Western Australia, do not, so check your city in the table below.

Nepalis in Australia often live far from relatives, and the cost of housing and study can push people into extra shifts and less sleep. Universities offer counselling and your GP can arrange a mental health treatment plan. An online session with us can sit alongside them, especially when you would like to talk in Nepali.

Common pressures for Nepalis in Australia

These are common experiences, not a diagnosis. If several feel familiar and have lasted for weeks, it is worth talking to someone.

  • Study, part-time work and the cost of living, all at once.
  • Visa and residency worries, and the pressure of a long process.
  • Loneliness and missing family, especially in the first years.
  • Feeling that you have to succeed because of what your family gave up.
  • Sleep problems from shifts and late-night study.

Frequently asked questions

Can I have a video consultation with a Nepali psychiatrist from Australia?

Yes. Dr. Kushal Kharel, founder of Kathmandu Mental Health Clinic and a Consultant Psychiatrist (MD Neuropsychiatry, NMC No. 27199), provides online psychiatry video consultation for Nepalis in Australia through Google Meet or WhatsApp video call, or by phone if video is not possible, in Nepali or English, and Dr. Sunita Lama and Dr. Bhishma K.C. also provide video consultation. Tell us which doctor you prefer when you book on WhatsApp or 9714556408; no referral is needed.

What is the time difference between Australia and Nepal?

Nepal is at UTC+5:45 all year. Sydney and Melbourne: in January Nepal is 5 h 15 min behind, in July Nepal is 4 h 15 min behind. Brisbane: in January Nepal is 4 h 15 min behind, in July Nepal is 4 h 15 min behind. Perth: in January Nepal is 2 h 15 min behind, in July Nepal is 2 h 15 min behind.

What should I do if I need urgent help in Australia?

Call the local emergency number now: 000. Crisis line: Lifeline 13 11 14. Kathmandu Mental Health Clinic is not an emergency service.

Can you prescribe medicine if I live in Australia?

A prescription from Nepal is generally not valid abroad, so the doctor explains what is possible where you live. Where medicine is needed, we can help you prepare a summary for a doctor in Australia, and we work with therapy and practical strategies in the meantime. A family member in Nepal can arrange payment for you if that is easier; we share the fee when you book.

I am an international student in Australia. Can I book around my classes?

Yes. Tell us your timetable and we will look for a slot that fits, in your local time. If you are in danger or thinking of ending your life, call 000, or Lifeline on 13 11 14, first.

Will Medicare or my student health cover pay for a session?

Cover for a doctor based outside Australia usually does not apply, so please check with your own provider. We share the fee and the payment options when you book, before your session, so you can decide.
