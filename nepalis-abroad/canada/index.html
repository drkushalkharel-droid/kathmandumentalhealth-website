Nepalis in Canada: life, work and study

Nepalis in Canada live in cities across the country, including Toronto, Calgary, Edmonton, Vancouver, Ottawa and Winnipeg. Many are international students, workers or new residents, and many families have built lives there over years. Long, cold winters can be hard on mood.

Canada has several time zones and changes its clocks in March and November.

Canada is vast and cold for much of the year, and many Nepali newcomers arrive as students in the middle of winter. University counselling and the 9-8-8 line are options alongside online psychiatry, and a first session with us can help you decide what kind of support you need.

Common pressures for Nepalis in Canada

These are common experiences, not a diagnosis. If several feel familiar and have lasted for weeks, it is worth talking to someone.

  • Study, work and the cost of living, all at once.
  • Residency and work-permit worries.
  • Long, cold, dark winters, which can lower mood for some people.
  • Loneliness and missing family and festivals at home.
  • Pressure to succeed for family back home.

Frequently asked questions

Can I have a video consultation with a Nepali psychiatrist from Canada?

Yes. Dr. Kushal Kharel, founder of Kathmandu Mental Health Clinic and a Consultant Psychiatrist (MD Neuropsychiatry, NMC No. 27199), provides online psychiatry video consultation for Nepalis in Canada through Google Meet or WhatsApp video call, or by phone if video is not possible, in Nepali or English, and Dr. Sunita Lama and Dr. Bhishma K.C. also provide video consultation. What is the time difference between Canada and Nepal?

Nepal is at UTC+5:45 all year. Toronto and Ottawa (Eastern): in January Nepal is 10 h 45 min ahead, in July Nepal is 9 h 45 min ahead. Calgary and Edmonton (Mountain): in January Nepal is 12 h 45 min ahead, in July Nepal is 11 h 45 min ahead. Vancouver (Pacific): in January Nepal is 13 h 45 min ahead, in July Nepal is 12 h 45 min ahead.

What should I do if I need urgent help in Canada?

Call the local emergency number now: 911. Crisis line: Call or text 9-8-8. Kathmandu Mental Health Clinic is not an emergency service.

Can you prescribe medicine if I live in Canada?

A prescription from Nepal is generally not valid abroad, so the doctor explains what is possible where you live. Where medicine is needed, we can help you prepare a summary for a doctor in Canada, and we work with therapy and practical strategies in the meantime. A family member in Nepal can arrange payment for you if that is easier; we share the fee when you book.

I am a student in Canada. Can I see a psychiatrist from Nepal?

Yes. You can meet our doctors by private video in Nepali or English, at times that fit around classes and work. If you are in danger or thinking of ending your life, call 911, or call or text 9-8-8.

Does my provincial health plan cover a session with a doctor in Nepal?

Provincial health plans generally do not cover care from a doctor outside Canada, so please check with your own plan. We share the fee and the payment options when you book, before your session.
