Nepalis in Oman: life, work and study

Oman has very hot summers, and many jobs are outdoors or in shifts. Some workplaces and homes are in small towns or remote areas, where other Nepalis, and clinics that speak Nepali, can be far away. That is one reason video care can help: you do not have to travel to be seen.

Oman uses one time zone all year, with no clock changes. The table below shows what your local time is in Nepal.

Oman's climate varies: Muscat and the interior are very hot in summer, while Dhofar, around Salalah, has a cooler, misty monsoon season. Wherever you live, regular sleep and meal times and steady contact with friends and family at home help mood, and a doctor can build those steps into a plan with you.

Common pressures for Nepalis in Oman

These are common experiences, not a diagnosis. If several feel familiar and have lasted for weeks, it is worth talking to someone.

  • Long shifts and heat, with little energy left afterwards.
  • Living in a small town or remote site, far from other Nepalis and from family.
  • Language barriers at work and when you need a doctor.
  • Contract and money worries, and the pressure to send money home.
  • Low mood, worry and poor sleep that build up over months.

Time difference between Oman and Nepal

Nepal is at UTC+5:45 all year, with no daylight saving.

Muscat and all of OmanNepal is 1 h 45 min ahead8 pm there = 9:45 pm in Nepal

The clocks in this country do not change during the year, and Nepal has no daylight saving either, so the gap stays the same.

Nepal is 1 h 45 min ahead of Oman. An evening after work in Oman is usually late evening in Nepal. If that is too late for you, tell us and we will look for the closest slot that works.

The weekend in Oman is Friday and Saturday, but many workers have only one day off a week, so tell us which day is yours. During Ramadan, working hours and sleep change in many workplaces, and we can plan around them.

Can you prescribe medicine if I live in Oman?

A prescription from Nepal is generally not valid abroad, so the doctor explains what is possible where you live. Where medicine is needed, we can help you prepare a summary for a doctor in Oman, and we work with therapy and practical strategies in the meantime. A family member in Nepal can arrange payment for you if that is easier; we share the fee when you book.

I live in a remote part of Oman with a weak signal. Can we still meet?

Yes. If the video keeps freezing, we can switch to a phone call so that you still get your session. Tell us roughly where you are when you book so we can plan for a weak connection.

Do you see Nepalis in Oman who work as domestic or care workers?

Yes. Every Nepali living or working in Oman can book, whatever their job. Tell us which hours you are free and whether it is hard to speak privately, and we will plan the session around that.
