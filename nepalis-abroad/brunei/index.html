Nepalis in Brunei: life, work and study

Brunei is a small country with a small Nepali community, which means that people who need support may not know anyone else in the same situation. Some Nepali families in Brunei are linked to the Gurkha community, and others come for work.

Brunei uses one time zone all year, with no clock changes.

Brunei is quiet and orderly, and for people used to busier places the evenings can feel long and isolated. If you are posted or working there on a fixed contract, planning regular calls with family and follow-up sessions can give you something to look forward to.

Common pressures for Nepalis in Brunei

These are common experiences, not a diagnosis. If several feel familiar and have lasted for weeks, it is worth talking to someone.

  • Being part of a very small community, with few people to talk to.
  • Long working hours and shift work.
  • Long postings or contracts, far from family.
  • Money worries, and the pressure to send money home.
  • Loneliness, low mood and poor sleep.

Time difference between Brunei and Nepal

Nepal is at UTC+5:45 all year, with no daylight saving.

Bandar Seri Begawan and all of BruneiNepal is 2 h 15 min behind8 pm there = 5:45 pm in Nepal

The clocks in this country do not change during the year, and Nepal has no daylight saving either, so the gap stays the same.

Can you prescribe medicine if I live in Brunei?

A prescription from Nepal is generally not valid abroad, so the doctor explains what is possible where you live. Where medicine is needed, we can help you prepare a summary for a doctor in Brunei, and we work with therapy and practical strategies in the meantime. A family member in Nepal can arrange payment for you if that is easier; we share the fee when you book.

Is there a mental health helpline in Brunei?

Talian Harapan (145) is a national helpline. For an emergency, call 993 for police, 991 for an ambulance or 995 for fire.

I am part of a Gurkha family in Brunei. Can I be seen in Nepali?

Yes. Sessions can be in Nepali, English or a mix of both, and we understand Nepali family life and culture. Partners and family members can join by video if you would like.
