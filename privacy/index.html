Legal

Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Your privacy matters, especially in mental health. This is how we handle your information.

In this article

  • What we collect
  • How we use it
  • Clinical information
  • Free tools
  • Third parties
  • Your choices

What we collect

When you call, WhatsApp or email us, we receive the details you share (such as your name, phone number and a short description of your concern). On this website we use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful. It uses cookies and collects usage data such as pages viewed, device type and approximate location. It does not collect your health details.

How we use it

We use what you share to reply, book appointments and provide care. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Clinical information

What you tell your doctor is confidential. It is shared with family, other doctors or services only with your consent, or where the law requires it, or where there is a serious risk to someone's life.

Free tools

The check-in, circles of control and other free tools store entries only in your own browser. We cannot see them. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Third parties

Calls and messages pass through your phone provider and WhatsApp. Video consultations use Google Meet or WhatsApp. Their own privacy policies apply to those services. Our website is hosted by a third-party provider.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or opt out of analytics cookies through your browser settings. Contact us on +977 9714556408.

This page is a plain-language summary and should be reviewed by the clinic and, if required, a legal adviser before relying on it.

Talk to a psychiatrist

Call or message us. We reply in Nepali or English, and all three of our doctors also consult online.
